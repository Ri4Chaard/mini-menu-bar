I want to build a small menu bar app for macOS that solves a few everyday annoyances for me, all in one place.

**Core problem:** Screenshots I take on macOS keep getting lost — they either end up scattered on the Desktop or saved to some folder I forget about. I want a dedicated place where I can always see my recent screenshots, at a glance, without hunting for them.

**What the app should do:**

The app lives in the menu bar (the top bar of the screen). Clicking its icon opens a small panel with a sidebar on the left for navigation between sections, and the content of the selected section on the right.

The sections I want:

1. **Screenshots** — shows my recent screenshots as thumbnails, so I never lose track of them again. I should be able to open or locate a screenshot from here.

2. **Timer** — a simple countdown timer I can start, pause, and get notified when it's done. I'd like to be able to start/control it with a keyboard shortcut too, without opening the app.

3. **Spotify** — control my currently playing music: see what's playing, play/pause, skip to next/previous track, and scrub through the track.

4. **Notes** — a simple place to jot down short personal notes, nothing fancy — just create, edit, and delete them.

**A key feature I care about:** for the sections where it makes sense (Screenshots, Timer, Spotify), I want the option to also show a compact, glanceable version of that section directly in the menu bar itself — for example, a small thumbnail of my latest screenshot with a counter of how many new ones I have, a live countdown for the timer, or the name of the currently playing song. Each of these should be toggled on/off independently by me, from within the app, so I can decide exactly what clutters my menu bar and what doesn't.

Notes don't need a menu bar preview — they only live inside the main panel.

**What I'm not looking for right now (but might want later):** a translator, calendar, weather, battery status, or performance monitoring (CPU/RAM). These are ideas for the future, not part of what I need built now.

**Overall feel:** this should feel like one lightweight, unified hub for these small daily tasks, rather than four separate apps — quick to open, quick to glance at, and easy to customize in terms of what's visible in the menu bar at any time.
